IntoExistence

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 June 2026

IntoExistence is a personal affirmation app. We built it to be private by default: we collect only what we need to run your account and your practice, we don't use analytics or advertising trackers, and we never sell your data.

This Privacy Policy explains what information IntoExistence ("the app", "we", "us"), operated by Jack Holdway, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to the IntoExistence mobile app.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and a password. Your password is never stored in readable form — it is hashed and managed by our authentication provider (Supabase). We use your email to sign you in, verify your account with a one-time code, and let you reset your password.

Content you create

We store the affirmation statements you write and a record of which statements you have completed each day. This is the core of the app — your statements and progress are saved to your account so they're there every time you sign in.

Subscription information

IntoExistence offers an auto-renewable subscription. All payments are processed by Apple — we never see or store your credit card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives a non-identifying app user ID (tied to your account) and purchase/receipt information from Apple so the app knows whether your subscription is active. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Information stored only on your device

Some settings never leave your phone. Your daily reminder time and whether you've finished onboarding are stored locally on your device only. Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device — we do not run a notification server and do not collect a push token.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party ad or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics SDKs (we don't track how you use the app).
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save and display your statements and daily progress.
  • To determine whether you have an active subscription.
  • To send the daily reminder you choose to enable.
  • To respond to support requests and protect against abuse or fraud.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

3. How your information is shared

We don't sell your data. We share information only with the service providers that make the app work, and only as needed to provide the service:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and authentication (stores your email, statements, and completion history). See supabase.com/privacy.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status (see above).
  • Apple — processes all subscription payments through the App Store.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the service.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app (More → Account → Delete Account). Deleting your account permanently removes your account and all of your statements and completion history; this cannot be undone. Cancelling a subscription is handled separately through your Apple ID settings and does not delete your account.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your session tokens are stored in the device's secure Keychain. Access to your data is restricted by row-level security so that you can only ever access your own statements and progress. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Children's privacy

IntoExistence is not directed to children. You must be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in your country) to use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. International users

Your information is stored and processed by our service providers, which may operate in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using the app, you understand your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can access and edit your statements directly in the app and delete all of your data by deleting your account. For any other request, email us and we'll help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above, and significant changes may be communicated in the app. Continued use of IntoExistence after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email intoexistenceapp@gmail.com.
